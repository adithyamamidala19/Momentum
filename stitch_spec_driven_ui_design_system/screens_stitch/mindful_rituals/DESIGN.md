## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a sanctuary of calm, intentional pacing, and grounded warmth. Built for ritual cultivation and reflective habit formation, it rejects gamified urgency, intrusive badges, and anxiety-inducing streaks. The target audience seeks space to breathe, reflect, and gently integrate daily practices into their lives without shame or digital overstimulation.

The visual style blends warm organic minimalism with serene editorial poise. Interfaces feel tactile yet weightless, relying on generous negative space, gentle hairline structure, quiet typographic hierarchies, and soothing natural tints. Rather than relying on skeuomorphism or modern glass effects, the interface evokes high-grade textured paper, natural ceramic washes, and unhurried editorial literature.

Key brand principles:
- **Unhurried simplicity:** Visual clutter, dense data density, and bright callouts are omitted. Every screen allows the content to rest.
- **Supportive neutrality:** Interfaces do not scold or judge. Warning alerts never use aggressive reds; they invite gentle care through understated amber outlines.
- **Pure sentence case:** All interface copy, buttons, tooltips, and headlines are set in natural sentence case. Title case, all caps, and exclamation marks are strictly forbidden.
- **Zero artificial gloss:** Avoid drop shadows, blurs, and neon gradients. Hierarchy is maintained through subtle tone shifts and fine-gauge structural lines.

## Colors
The color palette draws from grounding botanical greens, warm sunlit amber, and unbleached stone neutrals. It eliminates all saturated neons, high-contrast synthetic tones, and visceral warning reds.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#0F6E56`):** A muted, deep forest teal serving as the foundational accent for key actions, interactive states, and core progress indicators.
- **Primary Tint (`#E1F5EE`):** A soft water-mint wash used for container fills, active chip states, and peaceful visual groupings.
- **Reward / Milestone (`#BA7517`):** Warm amber applied deliberately to reflect completion, deepened practice, and milestone quiet celebrations.
- **Reward Tint (`#FAEEDA`):** A pale honey wash for subtle celebratory surfaces and milestone badges.
- **Attention / Needs-Care:** Represented strictly through `#BA7517` as a delicate hairline or dashed contour. Solid warning fills and red tones are prohibited across the product.
- **Canvas (`#FBFBFA`):** An organic warm off-white that prevents screen glare and sets a soothing baseline tone.
- **Surfaces:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary foreground cards and elevated content sheets, supplemented by warm stone gray (`#F4F4F2`) for nested sub-surfaces and tertiary wells.
- **Text & Stroke:** Deep charcoal (`#1C1D1C`) for primary readable content; soft warm slate (`#717672`) for secondary supporting metadata; neutral hairline divider stroke (`#E6E6E3`).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances the modern, humane legibility of Plus Jakarta Sans with the serene, contemplative qualities of Newsreader. 

### Typographic Hierarchy & Restraint
- **Structural UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Applied to functional interfaces, metrics, headings, buttons, and navigation. Headings use medium (500) weights to prevent visual tension. Body text uses regular (400) weight. Heavy bold weights (700+) are never used.
- **Contemplative Moments (`Newsreader`):** Reserved for inspirational aphorisms, daily mindful intentions, warm narrative reflections, and closing lines.
- **Copy Restrictions:** Sentence case is mandatory across every typographic tier. Emojis and exclamation marks must not be introduced into standard product typography.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout philosophy centers on expansive breathing room, quiet pacing, and deliberate rhythm. Content is structured to encourage contemplation, using whitespace as an active structural element rather than void space.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (Base to 599px):** Single-column layout with 1.25rem (`margin`) canvas inset and 1.25rem (`gutter`) vertical spacing between stacked ritual modules.
- **Tablet (600px to 1023px):** 6-column fluid structure with 1.5rem margins and gutters. Cards adapt to two-column rhythmic pairs.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed-width central reading track (max 720px for text-focused ritual reflections, max 1080px for ritual management dashboards). 12-column grid with 2rem (`gutter-desktop`) and 3rem (`margin-desktop`).

### Vertical Rhythm & Pacing
Vertical spacing between discrete ritual sections defaults to `space-xl` (2.5rem) to ensure clear separation without visual density. Elements within cards maintain generous internal padding (1.25rem to 1.5rem) to prevent claustrophobic groupings.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects drop shadows, multi-layered Z-indices, and glassmorphism blurs in favor of flat tonal layering and precise hairline definitions.

### Depth Methodology
- **Tonal Grounding:** Canvas depth is established by resting pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces or soft gray wells (`#F4F4F2`) directly upon the warm off-white canvas (`#FBFBFA`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Visual boundaries are created using single-pixel or 0.5px subtle borders (`#E6E6E3`). The borders are quiet, low-contrast edges that organize information without harsh delineation.
- **Zero Box-Shadows:** No box-shadow tokens exist within this system. All cards, popovers, and sheets rely solely on surface tint contrast and perimeter borders for separation.
- **Modals and Drawers:** Elevated overlays rest on a warm, semi-translucent scrim (`rgba(28, 29, 28, 0.25)`) accompanied by a 0.5px hairline border on the foreground surface.

## Shapes
Shapes are soft, organic, and gentle to touch, mirroring river stones and ceramic dishes. 

- **Containers & Cards:** Employ a 12px to 16px radius (`0.75rem` to `1rem`), providing a welcoming, non-aggressive perimeter.
- **Small Controls:** Inputs, status chips, and selector controls use a continuous 8px to 10px contour.
- **Pill Shapes:** Full pill radii are reserved strictly for interactive state indicators, minimal category filters, and primary pill action buttons.
- **Border Treatment:** Outlines are consistently rendered at 0.5px to 1px thickness with soft edge transitions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with `#0F6E56`, displaying white text (`#FFFFFF`) in `label-md`. Pill or soft rounded (12px), padded generously with 0.75rem vertical and 1.5rem horizontal spacing. Zero drop shadows.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 0.5px hairline border in `#E6E6E3` and text in `#1C1D1C`. On hover or press, transitions smoothly into a soft wash of `#F4F4F2`.
- **Milestone Action:** Filled with `#BA7517`, white text, deployed exclusively for completing significant milestones or finalizing long-form rituals.

### Chips & Tags
- **Passive Tags:** Background in `#F4F4F2`, text in `#717672`, 8px corner radius, no border.
- **Active / Selected:** Background in `#E1F5EE`, text in `#0F6E56`, 0.5px border in `#0F6E56`.
- **Needs-Care Indicator:** Transparent background, text in `#BA7517`, surrounded by an 0.5px dashed border in `#BA7517`. Never uses solid warning backgrounds.

### Cards & Ritual Modules
- Built on a pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 0.5px border (`#E6E6E3`) and a 16px corner radius.
- Padding is generous (1.5rem), allowing headlines, supporting descriptions, and completion meters to breathe.
- Nested modules sit on `#F4F4F2` with 12px corners and no inner border.

### Checkboxes & Ritual Toggles
- Rounded squares (6px radius) or circular meditation toggles (20px diameter).
- Unchecked: 0.5px border in `#717672`, transparent center.
- Completed / Checked: Smooth fill in `#0F6E56` housing an unhurried, clean white outline checkmark.

### Input Fields
- Flat background in `#FFFFFF` with a 0.5px border in `#E6E6E3` and 10px rounded corners.
- Placeholder text in `#717672`. 
- Focus state: Subtle 1px solid stroke in `#0F6E56`; no neon ring or drop shadow glow.

### Affirmation & Quote Modules
- Full-bleed or softly rounded inset card set with a subtle background wash of `#F4F4F2` or `#E1F5EE`.
- Features `Newsreader` italic/regular typography with generous line height for breathing exercises, mindful check-ins, or daily intentions.
- Free of decorative quotation marks; framed solely by negative space.

### Iconography
- Hand-drawn quality outline style, strictly 1.5px stroke weight, sized between 18px and 24px.
- Monochromatic per icon, colored using either `#0F6E56`, `#717672`, or `#BA7517`. No dual-tone fills or multicolored badges.
