1. Executive Summary & Design Philosophy

Momentum is a habit cultivation platform designed specifically to combat app burnout. Instead of anxiety-inducing streak breaks, gamified XP bloat, or guilt notifications, Momentum offers an unhurried digital sanctuary.

Key Invariants:

  • Zero Gamification Bloat: Strict exclusion of XP, badges, diamond tiers, and celebratory popups.
  • Spacious Concentric Circular Score Ring (160×160 viewBox): Multi-ring SVG with over 74px clear center clearance showing live adherence score without overlapping text.
  • Dynamic Practice Scoring Engine: Dynamically calculates adherence (0–100%) and cumulative practice points (1600+ base journey).
  • Interactive Habit Action Popover: Complete (+25 pts), Skip for Today (rest day without penalty), Mark Incomplete, and Cancel.
  • Web Audio Synthesizer: Ambient pentatonic pad (F Maj9) and singing bowls (396Hz/432Hz/528Hz) generated locally with zero audio file dependencies.
